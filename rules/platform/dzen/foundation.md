# Dzen

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Treat the article as a feed-discovered long-form artifact: the title, cover, first screen, and first paragraphs must make the reader promise clear without clickbait or unsupported curiosity gaps.
- Optimize for continuation, not only opening. The article should answer the reader's question early, then earn deeper reading with concrete sections, examples, images, and transitions.
- Preserve uniqueness and source integrity. Do not repackage copied material, generated filler, or lightly rewritten source text as original channel content.
- Keep layout compatible with a constrained editor: semantic headings, short paragraphs, lists, embedded media, captions, and links must carry the structure without relying on custom CSS.
- Verify current Studio limits before publication, including title length, tag count, cover aspect ratios, moderation restrictions, RSS behavior, monetization eligibility, and post-publication editing effects.
- Use cover media as a truthful preview of the article, not as documentary evidence unless it is sourced and rights-cleared. Avoid tiny text that becomes unreadable in feed cards.
- If the article is meant to build a channel, keep topic scope and author promise consistent across the piece; do not broaden into unrelated niches for reach.
- Separate commercial integrations, affiliate links, paid placement, and product ownership from editorial claims.
- Record platform-sensitive assumptions in the audit output when they affect title, cover, length, publishing cadence, monetization, or moderation risk.
