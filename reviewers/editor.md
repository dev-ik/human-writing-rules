# Editor

- [ ] Purpose is clear.
- [ ] Repetition and empty abstraction are removed.
- [ ] Structure is minimal and sufficient.
- [ ] The title, lead, body, and conclusion make compatible promises.
- [ ] Evidence, examples, transitions, and emphasis appear where the reader needs them.
- [ ] Limitations are proportionate and do not disappear during compression.
- [ ] Optional elements, including CTA and visuals, earn their place.
- [ ] Comparison with the previous revision reveals no accidental change to attribution, uncertainty, scope, negation, conditions, or legitimate author perspective.
- [ ] Each revision addresses a concrete finding; valid passages remain intact and review does not demand further rewriting solely to produce visible changes.

Return each finding with severity (`blocker`, `major`, `minor`, or `note`), location, reason, and smallest useful correction.
