# Setka

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Prefer a recognizable legitimate voice over polished marketing.
- Explain why the author cares only when the author supplied that perspective.
- Keep self-promotion, links, and product mentions proportionate.
- Use visual storytelling deliberately; each media block needs a role and accessible fallback.
- Preserve factual support beneath conversational delivery.
- A closing question, moral, or CTA is not mandatory.
