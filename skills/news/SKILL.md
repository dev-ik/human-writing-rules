# News Skill

Separate what happened, when, who confirms it, why it may matter, and what remains unknown.

## Workflow

1. Establish the event time, publication time, update time, and current cutoff.
2. Confirm the central development with the most direct available source.
3. Attribute disputed, exclusive, anonymous, or preliminary claims.
4. Add only context that changes interpretation.
5. Separate fact, reaction, analysis, forecast, and unresolved question.
6. Update the headline, lead, body, caption, and metadata together when facts change.

Do not inflate a statement into an event, repeat a rumor through circular citations, or use “breaking” after the information is stale.

Generated images must not depict an event as if they were photographs. Prefer verified documentary assets; label conceptual art clearly.
