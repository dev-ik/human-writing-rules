# Business Topic Foundation

- Prefer filings, audited reports, official product documentation, contracts, and attributable first-party statements for company facts.
- Treat company metrics, market size, customer counts, and growth claims according to their definitions and reporting period.
- Separate revenue, bookings, valuation, funding, profit, cash flow, and user metrics.
- Identify sponsorship, commercial relationships, affiliate incentives, and access conditions.
- Compare companies or products using consistent criteria and dates.
- Do not infer market leadership, customer satisfaction, or causation from promotional material.
- Mark forecasts, scenarios, and strategy interpretations as such.

Visualized metrics require sourced values, definitions, units, period, and a non-misleading scale.
