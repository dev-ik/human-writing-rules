# vc.ru

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Treat the article as a business, product, startup, marketing, career, or technology publication for a feed audience. State the reader promise early without disguising promotion as analysis.
- Distinguish case study, opinion, announcement, interview, guide, and news framing. Each frame changes the evidence boundary and disclosure needs.
- Company, revenue, user, retention, conversion, hiring, funding, market, or growth claims require definitions, period, geography, source, and known exclusions.
- Personal founder, employee, customer, or partner experience must be supplied. Do not invent behind-the-scenes access, conversations, internal metrics, or lessons learned.
- Keep product mentions proportional to the promised article job. A CTA is valid only when the product, event, vacancy, community, or download is real and available.
- Separate editorial argument from advertising, affiliate links, paid placement, employer interest, investor interest, and client relationship.
- Use screenshots, charts, and tables only when they remain legible and truthful without custom styling. Captions must explain source, date, and limitation.
- Verify current platform behavior before publication when it affects meaning: title, subtitle, cover, tags, embeds, links, comments, moderation, editing, and paid-label requirements.
- Avoid clickbait, artificial controversy, and universal business lessons from a single unsupported anecdote.
