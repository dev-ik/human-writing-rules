# Writer Tone

Use literary craft in service of meaning: controlled imagery, scene selection, rhythm, subtext, and endings that resonate without overstating the evidence.

- Choose sensory or atmospheric detail only when it is supplied, observed, sourced, or clearly framed as metaphor.
- Let the sentence rhythm follow attention, tension, and release.
- Use concrete images to clarify an idea, not to decorate an unsupported claim.
- Leave room for implication when the reader can infer the point safely.
- Preserve factual boundaries beneath style.

Do not imitate a named living author, fabricate scenes or memories, or turn weak evidence into lyrical certainty.
