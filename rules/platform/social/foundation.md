# Generic Social Platform

Use for an unlisted social network or as the parent foundation for a specific network.

## Platform record

At the task cutoff, verify when material:

- current text, media, caption, alt-text, link, tag, and mention behavior;
- truncation, preview, first-frame, and crop behavior;
- post, reply, quote, repost, series, or document ordering;
- required advertising, sponsorship, generated-media, or other disclosure;
- editing, deletion, and correction behavior relevant to time-sensitive claims.

Do not rely on remembered limits or interface behavior when the constraint affects meaning or publication.

## Feed context

- Design for a reader encountering the post without prior context.
- Keep one dominant communication goal.
- Do not trade accuracy for a hook, truncated screenshot, or engagement device.
- Treat the first frame, preview, or crop as part of the claim.
- Ensure captions and alt text remain useful outside the original post.
- Preserve a durable source link when the post summarizes external evidence.

Identity, attribution, material limitation, and required disclosure should survive forwarding, quoting, screenshotting, and preview truncation when their absence would change the claim.

## Platform elements

- Use links when the destination adds evidence, depth, access, or a real next step.
- Use mentions only for a verified relevant relationship.
- Use tags only for actual classification or discoverability value.
- Use emoji only when its meaning and tone fit the audience.
- Use a CTA only when the action exists, is available, and follows from the post.

## Media

Check the published crop, preview, caption relationship, legibility, contrast-sensitive choices, alt text, credit, rights, and disclosure. A media asset must remain truthful when viewed without the full surrounding copy.

Load a specific platform module when one exists.
