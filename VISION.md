# Vision

Human Writing Rules aims to become an open interoperability layer for AI-assisted writing.

The project standardizes an editorial process, not one universal style. Good writing may be formal, colloquial, technical, emotional, concise, or long-form. Compatibility means that choices are intentional, grounded, reviewable, and reproducible.

The primary product path is a sourced brief becoming a publication-ready article or social post. Visuals are optional artifacts within the same editorial package: they must have a defined purpose, remain consistent with the evidence, and pass their own review instead of being generated as decoration by default.

The specification separates artifact format, subject domain, publishing platform, task, tone, and author perspective. This allows the same scientific explainer to become a blog article, a Telegram post, or both without treating platform conventions as editorial intent.
