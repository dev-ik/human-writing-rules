# Education Topic Foundation

- Define the learner, prerequisite knowledge, learning objective, and observable result.
- Separate evidence about learning from the author's teaching preference.
- Sequence concepts by dependency, not by rhetorical drama.
- Include worked examples, verification, and common misconceptions when useful.
- Avoid promising mastery from exposure or completion alone.
- Provide accessible alternatives for media-dependent instruction.

Diagrams and illustrations must teach a defined concept; decorative visuals must not compete with the learning task.
