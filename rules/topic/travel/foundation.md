# Travel Topic Foundation

- Verify entry rules, transport, closures, safety notices, accessibility, seasonal conditions, and opening times with current authoritative sources.
- Date prices and availability; distinguish an example price from a reliable expectation.
- State the traveler's origin, timing, mobility, budget, and other assumptions when they affect advice.
- Separate firsthand experience from retrieved information.
- Avoid declaring a destination safe, cheap, authentic, or “undiscovered” without scope and evidence.
- Respect local communities, restrictions, and photography rules.

Maps, route diagrams, and location imagery must not imply verified access or conditions when they are merely illustrative.
