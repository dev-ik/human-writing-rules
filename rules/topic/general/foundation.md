# General Topic Foundation

Use for mixed, low-risk, or unlisted topics.

- Define material terms and boundaries.
- Identify which claims are factual, evaluative, experiential, or predictive.
- Prefer the most direct source available for each material claim.
- Record dates and scope when facts may vary over time or place.
- Surface meaningful disagreement and unknowns.
- Escalate to a specialist topic module when health, finance, legal status, safety, scientific evidence, or another high-risk domain becomes material.

General does not lower the source standard. It prevents unrelated specialist modules from being loaded.
