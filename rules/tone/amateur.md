# Amateur Tone

Use a curious non-specialist voice: honest questions, plain language, visible learning, and careful limits.

- Say what is clear, what is confusing, and what still needs checking.
- Prefer concrete examples over expert shorthand.
- Ask the reader's likely question before answering it.
- Keep uncertainty specific rather than vague.
- Admit scope limits without performing incompetence.

Amateur tone does not permit fake naivety, deliberate mistakes, invented learning journeys, or pretending the author lacks knowledge they actually used.
