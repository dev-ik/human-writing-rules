# Habr

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Select the publication type deliberately: article, news, translation, post, or tutorial marker. Do not publish a news item, press release, translation, or announcement as a regular article when the platform has a more accurate type.
- Match technical depth to the topic and intended hub or audience.
- Choose hubs and tags by the reader's real topic, not by reach. If the artifact is for a company blog, keep the company hub separate from technical hubs.
- Set the difficulty level from the reader prerequisite: simple for broad introductions, medium for readers with basic domain knowledge, and difficult for specialist material.
- Explain methodology, environment, versions, tradeoffs, and limitations.
- Do not disguise promotion as analysis or a vendor claim as independent testing.
- Include reproducible detail, code, configurations, and failure modes where useful.
- Verify code, diagrams, tables, labels, and screenshots.
- Separate a tutorial, case study, opinion, and announcement clearly.
- For translations, preserve the original author and source link as publication metadata and do not blur the boundary between translated text and editorial additions.
- For news, keep freshness, attribution, and the who/what/where/when facts ahead of interpretation; personal opinion belongs outside the news frame.
- Use headings, anchors, and summaries only when they help navigate a long article.
- Disclose employment, sponsorship, product ownership, or privileged access when material.
- Preview the publication before handoff: check markup, cut or preview text, cover behavior, images, tables, code blocks, formulas, links, polls, and mobile readability.
