# LinkedIn

Load `platform.social.foundation`.

## Professional context

- Establish professional relevance early without a theatrical hook.
- Metrics require definitions, period, baseline, and source.
- Personal experience, job history, customer reactions, and lessons must be real and supplied.
- Avoid engagement bait, invented consensus, line-by-line suspense, and forced vulnerability.
- Use mentions and tags only when the relationship is real and relevant.
- Keep linked-article context visible in the post.

Distinguish an individual's view, an employer or project position, customer evidence, and industry-wide claim. A professional tone does not authorize expertise, organizational representation, or access to private results.

## Claims and next steps

State availability, role, period, sample, and relevant boundary for hiring, product, customer, adoption, performance, or business claims. Do not turn a personal lesson into a universal management rule without evidence.

Use a CTA only when the job, event, release, article, repository, or other action is real and available to the stated audience.

## Links and media

Keep enough article, event, or release context in the post for feed reading. Verify the destination, mentions, image crop, embedded text, document or carousel order when used, captions, alt text, credit, and disclosure against current platform behavior.
