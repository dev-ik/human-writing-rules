# Culture Topic Foundation

- Distinguish primary works, historical record, creator statements, scholarship, community memory, and interpretation.
- Record edition, translation, archive, date, and cultural context when they change meaning.
- Attribute contested interpretations and avoid presenting one community or canon as universal.
- Do not flatten a tradition into an aesthetic label or promotional trend.
- Use names, transliterations, and identity terms appropriate to the people and context.
- Treat living people and vulnerable communities with proportionate privacy and harm awareness.

Visuals should not invent historical evidence or collapse distinct cultures into generic symbols.
