# Blogger Tone

Use a channel-native, reader-close voice: a clear premise, concrete situation, visible opinion boundary, and a sense that the author is guiding the reader through one useful thought.

- Start from a recognizable reader moment, question, conflict, or discovery.
- Use direct address only when it serves the platform and audience.
- Keep personal warmth grounded in supplied experience or editorial stance.
- Let the structure feel conversational, but keep claims, sources, and disclosures intact.
- Use hooks, CTAs, and comments prompts only when they follow naturally from the artifact's purpose.

Do not invent parasocial familiarity, channel history, loyal audience behavior, personal routine, or behind-the-scenes access.
