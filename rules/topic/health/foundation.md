# Health Topic Foundation

Health content is high-risk by default when it may influence diagnosis, treatment, medication, diet, or safety.

- Prefer current clinical guidelines, systematic reviews, regulator communications, and original research appropriate to the claim.
- State population, intervention, comparator, outcome, timeframe, and evidence quality.
- Distinguish laboratory, animal, observational, and clinical evidence.
- Report absolute risk or baseline context when relative changes could mislead.
- Do not diagnose, prescribe, promise outcomes, or replace professional care.
- Include urgent-care or emergency direction only from an authoritative, locale-appropriate source.
- Preserve contraindications, adverse effects, uncertainty, and conflicts of interest.

Generated medical imagery and diagrams must be clearly illustrative, anatomically reviewed when material, and never used as diagnostic evidence.
