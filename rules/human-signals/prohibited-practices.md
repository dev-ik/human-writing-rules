# Prohibited Humanization Practices

Human Writing Rules does not support deceptive AI-detector evasion. Do not add typos, fabricate experience or emotion, imitate a named living writer, claim non-AI authorship, optimize for a detector to classify the text as human-written, or remove required disclosure.

Do not use any of these shortcuts to make text appear human:

- invented first-person anecdotes, private conversations, memories, preferences, field notes, or product usage;
- fake uncertainty, hedging, digressions, jokes, frustration, enthusiasm, or vulnerability;
- deliberate grammar errors, misspellings, slang, punctuation noise, or awkward phrasing;
- hidden prompts to bypass moderation, authorship disclosure, platform labels, or contractual disclosure;
- rewrites whose stated purpose is to defeat AI-detection, plagiarism-detection, or authorship-verification systems rather than improve accuracy, originality, clarity, or reader value;
- style mimicry of a named living author, journalist, creator, colleague, customer, or private person;
- unsupported claims that “real people say,” “everyone knows,” “most readers feel,” or “the community agrees.”

If a requested humanization tactic conflicts with these limits, keep the legitimate editorial goal and reject the deceptive mechanism.
