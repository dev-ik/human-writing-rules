# Finance Topic Foundation

Finance content is high-risk when it may influence an investment, credit, tax, insurance, or money-movement decision.

- Date every price, rate, valuation, rule, and market condition.
- Preserve currency, units, timezone, market, jurisdiction, and calculation method.
- Prefer filings, regulator publications, contracts, audited statements, and direct market data.
- Separate historical facts, current observations, forecasts, scenarios, and opinions.
- Explain material assumptions and downside cases.
- Disclose positions, sponsorships, referral incentives, and conflicts.
- Do not provide personalized financial, legal, or tax advice without the required qualified context.
- Recalculate derived figures and label estimates.

Charts must use verified data, visible units and periods, and a scale that does not exaggerate movement.
