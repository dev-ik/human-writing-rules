# Product Launch Skill

Explain what is launching, who it serves, why it exists, what is available now, and material limitations.

## Inputs

- verified product identity and availability;
- intended users and problem;
- current capabilities and exclusions;
- release status, access conditions, price, version, and date;
- evidence for demonstrations or comparisons;
- author or company relationship to the product.

## Workflow

1. Separate shipped capability, beta behavior, roadmap, and aspiration.
2. Choose one reader problem and the most relevant capabilities.
3. Explain how the release changes the reader's options.
4. Include limitations, access, and next step proportionately.
5. Adapt the same evidence boundary to the selected format and platform.

Avoid revolutionary claims, invented demand, fabricated customer reactions, feature dumping, false urgency, and pretending a beta is complete.

For visuals, show a verified interface or workflow only from approved assets. A generated conceptual cover must not imply a product screen or capability that does not exist.
