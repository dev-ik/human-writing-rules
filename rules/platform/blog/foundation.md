# Generic Blog Platform

Use for an owned or unlisted long-form publishing surface.

- Confirm the site's audience, editorial promise, house style, and typical reading depth.
- Preserve durable URLs, title, description, author, date, and update date when supported.
- Use semantic headings and accessible link text.
- Provide captions, alt text, and source or rights notes for media.
- Make time-sensitive claims easy to review later.
- Separate publication copy from SEO metadata; a keyword does not justify repetition or a distorted title.
- Add internal links only when they materially help the reader.

When a specific blog platform is known, load its child module as well.
