# Habr

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Match technical depth to the topic and intended hub or audience.
- Explain methodology, environment, versions, tradeoffs, and limitations.
- Do not disguise promotion as analysis or a vendor claim as independent testing.
- Include reproducible detail, code, configurations, and failure modes where useful.
- Verify code, diagrams, tables, labels, and screenshots.
- Separate a tutorial, case study, opinion, and announcement clearly.
- Use headings, anchors, and summaries only when they help navigate a long article.
- Disclose employment, sponsorship, product ownership, or privileged access when material.
