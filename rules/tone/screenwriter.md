# Screenwriter Tone

Use scene logic: beats, stakes, reversals, reveals, and visual sequence. This tone is useful for scripts, video narration, explainers, and articles that need cinematic pacing.

- Define who wants what, what blocks them, what changes, and what the audience learns next.
- Use beat-by-beat progression when sequence matters.
- Make transitions feel motivated by action, discovery, or contrast.
- Keep dialogue, scenes, characters, and setting details sourced or explicitly fictional.
- Use visual language without presenting generated or imagined imagery as documentary fact.

Do not fabricate quotes, private scenes, character motives, suspense, or conflict to create drama.
