# Telegram

Load `platform.social.foundation`.

## Message design

- Front-load the subject, not manufactured suspense.
- Use paragraph breaks for comfort and preserve one dominant idea per post.
- Keep enough evidence and qualification in the post for forwarded or copied reading.
- Use links with a clear reason to follow them.
- Emojis, reactions, hashtags, and closing questions are optional.
- Avoid forced CTA endings and artificial “continued below” pacing.

Choose one message when the promise and material boundary fit. For a series, define the overall promise, make order explicit, give each unit one role, and keep identity and important qualification recoverable when a unit is forwarded alone.

## Links and distribution

Check the link destination, generated preview, publication time, and whether the post remains useful if the preview is absent. Distinguish channel voice, named author perspective, quoted source, and sponsored or partner material.

Forwarding can remove channel history, comments, and neighboring messages. Do not place essential correction, attribution, or disclosure only in those locations.

## Media

- Check the actual preview and crop of attached media.
- Supply useful alt text or an accessible caption when the publishing workflow supports it.

Also verify attachment order, caption-to-asset relationship, embedded text, credit, provenance, disclosure, and meaning when media is saved or forwarded separately.
