# Lifestyle Topic Foundation

- Separate the author's real experience, product claims, expert guidance, and general recommendation.
- State who a recommendation may or may not suit.
- Verify safety, health, price, availability, and compatibility claims when material.
- Date recommendations that depend on current products or services.
- Avoid universal routines, guaranteed transformations, and invented personal anecdotes.
- Disclose sponsorship, gifts, affiliate links, and access conditions.

Use visuals to demonstrate a verified process or establish tone, not to fabricate a before-and-after result.
