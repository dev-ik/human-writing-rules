# Topic Reviewer

- [ ] The selected topic foundation matches the material claims.
- [ ] Domain-specific source types, dates, versions, populations, or jurisdictions are adequate.
- [ ] Specialist terms are accurate and explained at the audience's level.
- [ ] Domain uncertainty, disagreement, limitations, and conflicts are proportionate.
- [ ] High-risk claims receive the required qualification or escalation.
- [ ] Topic conventions do not override the article's actual evidence.

Return each finding with severity (`blocker`, `major`, `minor`, or `note`), location, reason, and smallest useful correction.
