# Newsletter

Load `platform.blog.foundation`.

- Treat the artifact as an inbox-native issue. Define the subscriber promise, issue purpose, cadence assumption, and whether the reader already has context.
- Draft subject line, preview text, body, link labels, and closing action as separate surfaces. Do not let the subject overclaim what the body proves.
- Put the main value before housekeeping. If the issue is a digest, separate original editorial judgment from summarized source claims.
- Preserve source links, dates, author attribution, sponsorship, affiliate relationships, generated-media disclosure, and correction policy when material.
- Keep the issue useful when forwarded: essential context, identity, limitations, and disclosure should survive outside the original list relationship.
- Avoid false intimacy, invented community consensus, fake scarcity, and urgency that is not supported by a real deadline.
- For product, event, or paid newsletters, state availability, eligibility, pricing boundary, and cancellation or access assumptions when relevant.
- Check current provider constraints before publication: subject length, preview text, clipping, image blocking, alt text, unsubscribe footer, tracking settings, and archive visibility.
- Use segmentation or personalization only from authorized data. Do not imply knowledge of the reader that was not supplied.
