# Russian Language Foundation

Write directly in Russian. Do not preserve English sentence architecture when it sounds unnatural.

Common risks: bureaucratic noun chains, excessive introductory phrases, literal English hooks, inflated novelty claims, uniform short paragraphs, and unnecessary em dashes.

Prefer concrete verbs and natural paragraph length. Avoid “В современном мире”, “Важно отметить”, and “Давайте разберёмся” unless genuinely necessary. Do not simulate familiarity.
